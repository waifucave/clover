# Plan my day

1. Ask what's on their plate today, unless they already said. Two or three quick questions at most: deadlines, meetings, the one thing that must get done.
2. Sort it into: must do today, should do, can wait. Keep each item to a few words.
3. Suggest an order for the must-dos, with rough times if they gave you any. Leave breathing room.
4. Pin the top three to your stage with pin_note (one note each), so they stay in view. Unpin each one when they finish it, and celebrate when the last one goes.
5. Offer to check in later. Don't set up a routine unless they ask for one.

Keep it short and warm. A plan they'll follow beats a perfect one.
