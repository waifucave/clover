# Explain it like a friend

1. Read what they pointed at first (the file, the error, the page). Don't explain what you haven't read.
2. Start with one sentence: what it is, in plain words.
3. Then why it matters to them, here, now.
4. Then how it works, in three or four short steps at most. An everyday comparison helps if it's honest.
5. End with the one thing worth remembering, and offer to go deeper.

No jargon you don't explain, no walls of text. If you pin anything to your stage, pin that one thing to remember.
